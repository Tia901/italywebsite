Places to visit in Italy

Rome

  • Visit the Colosseum.
  • Make a wish at the Trevi Fountain.
  • Explore Vatican City.
  • Enjoy traditional Italian food.

Cinque Terre

  • Explore the five colorful villages along the Italian coast.
  • Try one of the scenic coastal hiking trails.
  • Try local food and the famous Ligurian pesto.

Amalfi Coast

  • Relax along the stunning Mediterranean coastline.
  • Visit beautiful towns such as Amalfi and Positano.
  • Take a scenic drive along the famous coastal road.
  • Try local lemon-based foods and drinks.

Venice

  • Explore the famous canals and take a gondola ride.
  • Visit St. Mark's Square and St. Mark's Basilica.
  • Walk across the beautiful Rialto Bridge.
  • Enjoy Venice's colorful streets, shops, and Italian restaurants.

Florence

  • Visit the magnificent Florence Cathedral (Duomo).
  • See famous artworks at the Uffizi Gallery.
  • Walk across the historic Ponte Vecchio bridge.
  • Explore Florence's beautiful squares, streets, and museums.

Naples

  • Try authentic Neapolitan pizza, famous around the world.
  • Visit the historic city center and its narrow streets.
  • Explore the ancient ruins of Pompeii nearby.
  • Enjoy views of Mount Vesuvius and the Bay of Naples.
